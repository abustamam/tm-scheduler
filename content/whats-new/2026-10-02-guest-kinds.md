---
title: Record what kind of guest someone is
date: 2026-10-02
audience: admins
public: false
link: /admin/vp-membership
---
On **VP Membership**, choose **Edit** from a guest's three-dots menu to record their **Kind** (Visitor, Visiting Toastmaster or Guest speaker), their **Home club** if they aren't a Visitor, and who they were **Introduced by**. A visiting Toastmaster's or guest speaker's kind and home club show on the agenda when they hold a role and in the attendance roll, and **Visitors introduced** counts how many guests each member brought.
