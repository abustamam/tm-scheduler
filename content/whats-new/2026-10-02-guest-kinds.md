title: Record what kind of guest someone is
date: 2026-10-02
audience: admins
public: false
link: /admin/vp-membership
---
Choose **Edit** from a guest's **⋯** menu on **VP Membership** to record their **Kind** (Visitor, Visiting Toastmaster or Guest speaker), their **Home club** and who they were **Introduced by**. A guest's kind and home club then show on the agenda and in the attendance roll, and **Visitors introduced** counts how many guests each member brought.
