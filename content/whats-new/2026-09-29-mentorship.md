title: Pair new members with a mentor
date: 2026-09-29
audience: everyone
public: false
link: /dashboard
---
Admins can now pair a member with a mentor from the member's page, with an optional focus like new member, contest or leadership. Mentors and mentees see each other, with contact details, on their dashboards, and admins see every pairing and who doesn't have a mentor yet. Members can tick "I'm willing to mentor" on their dashboard so the VP Education knows who to ask. New members' first-weeks checklist gains a "Get a mentor" step that ticks itself once they're paired.
