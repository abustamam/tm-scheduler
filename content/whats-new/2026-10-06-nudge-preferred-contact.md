---
title: Nudges lead with how a member likes to be reached
date: 2026-10-06
audience: admins
public: false
---
Next to a role holder on a meeting's agenda, the nudge buttons now lead with the member's preferred contact method, marked **Preferred**, and members who prefer **Call** or **SMS** get a button for it beside the usual WhatsApp and email ones. Call opens your dialer and SMS opens your messaging app with the message ready; GavelUp drafts it and you send it.
