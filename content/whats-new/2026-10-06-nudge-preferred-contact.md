A nudge now leads with the member's preferred contact method, marked **Preferred**, and adds **Call** and **SMS** buttons for members who prefer them, alongside WhatsApp and email. Call opens your dialer and SMS opens your messaging app with the message ready; GavelUp drafts it and you send it.
