A connected AI assistant, still in Beta, can now edit a meeting's agenda from a plain-language request, read its run sheet (each row's start time and when the meeting ends) and read the feedback notes left for you. It previews an edit before applying it; the assistants you've connected are listed under **Connected apps** in **Account settings**.
