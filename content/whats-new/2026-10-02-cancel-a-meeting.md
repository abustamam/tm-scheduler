---
title: Cancel a meeting, and restore it
date: 2026-10-02
audience: admins
public: false
link: /schedule
---
Officers can skip a meeting with **Cancel meeting** on its page and bring it back with **Restore** until the end of that day. A cancelled meeting is locked and everyone keeps their role; GavelUp sends nothing, but **View notice** drafts a message naming the date and every role holder, for you to send.
