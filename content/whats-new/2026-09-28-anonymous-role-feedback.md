---
title: Leave a role holder an anonymous note
date: 2026-09-28
audience: everyone
public: true
---
Anyone at a meeting, signed in or not, can open **Leave feedback** on the meeting page from the meeting's start until three days after it ends, pick a person and the role they filled, and write them a short note: what went well and one thing to try. The note has no author attached, and the person reads it once the meeting is over.
