Open **Agendas** to make the standard agenda your club's own. New meetings then start on your club's default agenda instead of GavelUp's, and upcoming meetings that are still on the standard agenda move to it. Meetings you have already changed, or where members have signed up for a role the new default leaves out, keep their agenda, and the page lists them so you can decide. To change the default, edit any meeting's agenda and save it as a club template, then set that template as the default.
