---
title: Every role now comes with a before-and-during guide
date: 2026-09-30
audience: everyone
public: true
---
Each meeting role now says what to do before the meeting and what to do in the room. When you hold a role, your own meeting page shows the prep first and switches to the in-meeting steps on the day, with a link to the printable script for roles that have one. Guests see the same guide on the club's meeting roles page. Admins can rewrite the guide for any role, including roles the club added itself, from the meeting roles settings.
