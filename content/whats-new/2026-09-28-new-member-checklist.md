date: 2026-09-28
audience: everyone
public: false
link: /dashboard
---
New members now get a short checklist on their dashboard: choose a path, schedule an Ice Breaker, take a supporting role, and set up Base Camp. It ticks itself off as they sign up, and disappears when they're done or say they're all set. Admins can start it for anyone from their member page. Members brought in by a roster import or a pasted list don't get it automatically.
