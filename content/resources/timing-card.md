# Timing color-card reference

Every speaking segment in a Toastmasters meeting has a target time, and the
Timer signals how you're doing with three colored cards (or lights): **green**,
**yellow**, and **red**. Learning to read them keeps the meeting on schedule and
helps you finish strong.

## What the colors mean

- **Green** — you've reached the *minimum* time. You're in the target window; keep going.
- **Yellow** — you're near the end. Start wrapping up your point.
- **Red** — you've hit the *maximum* time. Bring your remarks to a close now.

## The 30-second grace period

The colors are guidance; the **qualifying window** is the rule. A speech
qualifies from **30 seconds before green** through **30 seconds after red** —
half a minute of grace at each end. Finish outside that window and the speech is
disqualified from the vote (and, in a contest, from the results).

So a 5–7 minute speech qualifies anywhere between **4:30 and 7:30**. Green at
5:00 and red at 7:00 are still the times to aim for — the grace period is what
the Timer uses to decide whether a speech counts, not a licence to run long.

## Typical timing windows

Exact times vary by segment and are set by your club — or, for contests, by the
official rulebook — but the common defaults are:

| Segment | Green | Yellow | Red | Qualifies between |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ice Breaker | 4:00 | 5:00 | 6:00 | 3:30 – 6:30 |
| Prepared speech | 5:00 | 6:00 | 7:00 | 4:30 – 7:30 |
| Evaluation | 2:00 | 2:30 | 3:00 | 1:30 – 3:30 |
| Table Topics answer | 1:00 | 1:30 | 2:00 | 0:30 – 2:30 |

Your printed agenda shows the same thing for the segments actually on it: the
green·yellow·red trio next to each speaker, each evaluator and the Table Topics
segment, and the qualifying window in the timing key. The Table Topics trio is
the window for one response, not for the whole segment.

## A tip for speakers

Ask the Timer beforehand where they'll sit, and glance up for the green card so
you know you've earned your minimum. Practicing with a timer at home is the
single easiest way to nail your window on the day.
