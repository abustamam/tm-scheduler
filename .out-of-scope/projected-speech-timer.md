# A live speech timer on the projected deck

We do not put an automatic green/yellow/red clock on present-mode slides.

## Why this is out of scope

Timer is a real Toastmasters functionary role that members sign up for and learn
from. A projected clock that runs itself makes that person redundant at the
meeting. The principle, settled in July 2026: keep human roles human. The same
principle governs `automatic-open-role-nudges.md`.

Nothing is missing for the human Timer. The green, yellow and red marks are
already computed from the run sheet (`TimingMarks`) and printed on the agenda
and the Timer's role sheet.

## What covers the need

The Timer-controlled form: a live stopwatch on the Timer's own phone (#729),
the Timer's measured times recorded into the minutes (#730), both on the slot
and timer resolver refactor (#732). If a phone-to-deck channel is ever built
(#580), it can mirror the Timer's display to the room, still under the Timer's
control.

## Reopen only as

Something the Timer controls or a club opts into, never a default clock on the
slide.
